Paredo privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026. This policy covers Paredo for QuickBooks Online, provided by Spring Harbor Studios.

Privacy at a glance

  • QuickBooks information stays local. Page content is processed in your browser to adjust the display, not sent to Spring Harbor Studios or our billing providers.
  • No analytics in the extension. The public website uses Google Analytics to measure visits and PostHog to measure landing-page visits and Add to Chrome clicks. We do not sell user data.
  • Your choices stay in your browser. Display preferences are saved locally in your Chrome profile.
  • Billing is handled separately. ExtensionPay and Stripe handle trial, subscription and payment information. Provider identity and status can sync through Chrome.

The sections below explain these practices, support messages and your choices in more detail.

QuickBooks page information

The extension reads the structure and visible content of supported QuickBooks Online pages in your browser to identify screens and adjust their display. This can involve financial information already displayed on those pages. The extension does not send QuickBooks page content, transaction details, balances or customer records to Spring Harbor Studios, ExtensionPay or Stripe.

Preferences and access status

Display preferences and a minimal subscription/trial access cache are stored locally in your Chrome profile. Preferences apply across QuickBooks companies within that profile. The extension uses no analytics or advertising trackers and does not sell user data.

Trials, subscriptions and payments

ExtensionPay handles email-based trial activation, purchase restoration and subscription status. Its bundled library stores activation identity and status using Chrome sync storage, with local storage as a fallback. Chrome sync may transfer that provider data between signed-in browsers according to your Chrome settings. Display preferences remain local.

The extension contacts ExtensionPay to verify access and opens provider-hosted pages when you start a trial, subscribe, restore access or manage a subscription. ExtensionPay and Stripe process the email, payment and billing information needed for those services. Card details are entered in hosted checkout, not in the extension. Spring Harbor Studios may access billing/customer information through provider dashboards for support, subscription administration and refunds.

See ExtensionPay’s privacy information and Stripe’s privacy policy.

Why the extension needs access

Access to qbo.intuit.com lets Paredo recognize supported screens and change their display as you navigate. Storage access saves preferences and trial/subscription status. Access to extensionpay.com supports activation and access verification. Paredo does not request QuickBooks API access or browser-history permission.

All executable extension code, including the bundled ExtensionPay library, ships in the extension package. Paredo does not download and run remote code. Provider-hosted billing pages are separate websites opened when you choose a billing action.

Support and retention

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Avoid including financial records or unredacted screenshots. We use support messages to respond and resolve issues, retaining them as needed for support and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Billing providers retain records under their own policies and legal obligations.

Your choices

You can change display preferences, turn Paredo off, or uninstall it. Uninstalling does not cancel a paid subscription or necessarily remove provider data held in Chrome sync. Use Manage subscription to cancel. To request deletion of support or account-related information, email support@springharborstudios.com; some billing records may need to be retained by providers or for legal obligations.

Website and policy changes

This public website uses Google Analytics to understand website visits and interactions. Google receives information such as pages visited, referring websites, browser/device information, and approximate location, and may store cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions. This website measurement is separate from the Paredo extension, which contains no analytics and does not send your QuickBooks page content to Google Analytics. We do not deliberately send names, email addresses, or QuickBooks records as analytics event data. See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on for more information and an opt-out option. The landing page also uses PostHog, hosted in the United States, to measure page visits and Add to Chrome clicks, including which button was used. PostHog receives page and referral URLs, browser/device information, technical request information and a pseudonymous browser identifier stored in cookies/local storage. Session replay and automatic interaction capture are disabled; we do not identify visitors by name or email. PostHog is used only on the public landing page, not in the extension or QuickBooks. See PostHog’s privacy policy. Browser tracking protection or content blockers may prevent collection. The website has no signup forms or third-party font requests. Your browser sends normal requests to GitHub Pages when you visit, including requests for locally hosted screenshot images. The website doesn’t automatically receive your QuickBooks data. Clicking a Store, billing-provider, or other external link takes you to that service; emailing support sends the information you choose to include. GitHub Pages hosts this website and handles technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. We will update this page when our data practices change.
