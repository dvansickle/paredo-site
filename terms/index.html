What you receive

Paredo is a Chrome extension that adjusts selected QuickBooks Online interfaces. A subscription covers one person across their companies and browser installations. You need your own access to QuickBooks Online. Supported layouts and compatibility may change as QuickBooks Online changes. This product does not provide accounting, tax or financial advice.

Trial

A 14-day trial begins when you confirm the trial email through ExtensionPay. No payment card is required. The trial does not automatically convert to a paid subscription. Recovering a trial with the same email retains its original end date.

Price and renewal

The subscription costs US$24 per year, plus any applicable taxes disclosed at checkout. Annual billing starts when you purchase and renews automatically until canceled. Payments and subscription management are provided through ExtensionPay and Stripe. Any future price changes will be communicated before they apply to your renewal.

Cancellation and access

Open Manage subscription in the Paredo menu to cancel future renewals. Cancellation normally preserves access through the paid term. Uninstalling the extension does not cancel billing. If you cannot reach subscription management, contact support@springharborstudios.com. When trial or subscription access ends, Paredo returns supported pages to native QuickBooks behavior and keeps local display preferences for later recovery.

30-day refunds

You may request a full refund within 30 days of your initial purchase or any renewal. Email support@springharborstudios.com using the email associated with the purchase. Do not send card numbers. We will process eligible requests through the payment provider; bank processing times vary. Refunded subscription access may end. Statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Privacy and support

See our product privacy policy. For compatibility problems or purchase restoration, contact support@springharborstudios.com. We aim to respond within two business days.

Independent product

Spring Harbor Studios supplies and supports this extension independently of Intuit and Google. We do not guarantee uninterrupted availability or compatibility with every QuickBooks layout. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.
