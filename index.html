Full privacy policy ↗
What it reads
The structure and visible content of supported QBO pages—including displayed names, balances, and transaction text—to recognize screens and adjust their layout.
What it stores
Your display choices and a small trial/subscription access cache in your Chrome profile. It doesn’t save copies of your QuickBooks records.
What leaves the browser
No QuickBooks page content is sent to us or our billing providers. Billing is separate: ExtensionPay verifies access, Stripe processes payments, and billing identity/status may sync through Chrome. This public website uses Google Analytics to measure visits; its host also receives normal web requests. The extension contains no analytics.

A focused tool, by choice

Who Paredo isn’t for.

If you’re an accountant or power user looking for navigation shortcuts, company switching, or batch transaction tools, start with RightTool. Its broader workflow toolkit may fit you better.

Paredo focuses on the view around your bookkeeping. It won’t automate accounting decisions, repair bank connections, or replace QuickBooks. If your current setup already works well, you may not need it.

Your books stay your books

A different view.
The same source of truth.

Paredo adjusts how supported pages look in your browser. It doesn’t post, categorize, or edit your accounting records.

QuickBooks page content, balances, and customer records aren’t sent to Spring Harbor Studios or our billing providers. There are no extension analytics or advertising trackers.

Display preferences stay local to your Chrome profile. ExtensionPay handles trial and subscription access; Stripe processes payments. Billing identity and status are handled separately from your QuickBooks data.
