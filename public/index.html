Your books stay your books

A different view.
The same source of truth.

Paredo adjusts how supported pages look in your browser. It doesn’t post, categorize, or edit your accounting records.

QuickBooks page content, balances, and customer records aren’t sent to Spring Harbor Studios or our billing providers. There are no extension analytics or advertising trackers.

Display preferences stay local to your Chrome profile. ExtensionPay handles trial and subscription access; Stripe processes payments. Billing identity and status are handled separately from your QuickBooks data.
